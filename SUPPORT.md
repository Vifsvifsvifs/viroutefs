# Support ViRouteFS

ViRouteFS is free software.

Support options are being prepared, but donations are not available through this repository yet. No crypto addresses, personal wallets, or sponsor links are published at this time.

Future support may include:

- Boosty.
- DonationAlerts.
- Crypto donations.
- Fiscal-host or open-source funding platforms where legally available.

Donations will be voluntary and will not unlock private features. ViRouteFS should remain local-first free software with no paid tracking, no ads, and no cloud dependency.
